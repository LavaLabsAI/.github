<h1 align="center">Lava Labs</h1>
<p align="center"><b>Advancing Safe AI. Strengthening Cyber Defense.</b></p>

Lava Labs is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit think tank focused on AI safety and cybersecurity research. We help people and institutions adopt AI more safely, responsibly, and effectively by studying implementation risks and cybersecurity challenges, then turning that work into research, education, and practical public guidance.

### What we do

- **Research:** study AI tools, safety implications, and the cybersecurity risks that come with real-world adoption.
- **Guidance:** turn research into practical frameworks, reports, and recommendations.
- **Education:** provide public-interest resources that help organizations and communities adopt AI more safely.
- **Policy engagement:** contribute research-informed perspectives to public discussions on responsible AI and cyber resilience.

### Who we serve

Small businesses, nonprofits, public institutions, schools, startups, security practitioners, and underserved communities.
